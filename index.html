// PRODUCT DESIGNER MANIFESTO

In complex software, the interface is merely the surface layer. True product design starts with business logic, multi-user permissions, and real-world edge cases.

In enterprise B2B software and on-demand mobility, 90% of user experience happens beneath the visual layer: multi-tier approval chains, compliance rules, and preventing catastrophic user mistakes before they happen. Get that structural logic right, and the interface becomes effortless.

Over 2+ years shipping at Digylax and building commercial platforms like Travel by Toshiv, I work shoulder-to-shoulder with engineering leads to ensure design systems are reusable, componentized, and strictly buildable.
